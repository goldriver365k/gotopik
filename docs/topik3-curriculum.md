# TOPIK 3 Curriculum Design (Stage 22)

Design-only document. No code, no React changes, no data files this
stage — this fixes the plan that later content-authoring stages will
follow, the same role `content-rules.md` plays for TOPIK 1~2.

TOPIK 2 completion is never a condition for TOPIK 3 access. All Levels
stay Open — this document only plans STEP content, not gating.

## 1. Level goal

TOPIK 3 moves learners past basic daily-life Korean toward early
intermediate: understanding somewhat longer sentences, expressing
reasons and opinions, reading everyday/social information, following
short spoken explanations, and writing simple sentences of their own.

In scope: longer sentences, wider vocabulary, some social (not just
personal daily-life) topics, more combined grammar, longer
Reading/Listening, and the start of Writing.

Out of scope for now: advanced argumentative writing, specialized
social-science topics, highly abstract vocabulary, long essays, AI
essay grading.

## 2. Structure

16 STEPs. Study flow per STEP:

```
WORDS → SENTENCES → GRAMMAR → LISTENING → READING → WRITING → PRACTICE → COMPLETE
```

WRITING is a new section in the flow, but its screen/UI is **not**
built this stage — see §6. Until it exists, StepContent simply omits
`writing`, exactly like TOPIK 1~2 do today.

## 3. STEP-by-STEP curriculum

General STEP: ~18–22 new core words, 6–8 sentences, 1–2 new grammar
points, 3 Listening, 3 Reading, 10–12 Practice, Writing per §5.
Vocabulary exposure target across a STEP's content (words + sentences
+ reading + listening combined): ~30–40% new, ~60–70% review/reuse —
TOPIK 1~2 vocabulary keeps getting reused, never re-registered as
"new".

| STEP | Topic | Learning goal | Grammar direction | Writing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 경험과 최근 일 (Recent experiences) | Describe a recent experience; connect a past experience to its result; talk about things tried before | -아/어 본 적이 있다, -고 나서 | 2-3 sentences: something done recently |
| 2 | 계획과 목표 (Plans and goals) | Describe upcoming plans; say what one will do to reach a goal | -(으)려고 하다, -기 위해서 | 2-3 sentences: this week/month's plan |
| 3 | 이유와 결과 (Reasons and results) | State a reason; understand why a result occurred | -기 때문에, -아서/어서 | 2-3 sentences including a reason |
| 4 | 비교와 선택 (Comparison and choice) | Compare two things; explain a choice | -보다, -중에서, -는 것이 더 | Pick one of two things and give a reason |
| 5 | 문제와 해결 (Problems and solutions) | Describe a problem; propose a solution | -(으)면, -는 게 좋다 | 3 sentences: problem + solution |
| 6 | 건강과 생활관리 (Health management) | Describe health habits; understand/give advice | -도록, -는 것이 좋다 | Something done for one's health |
| 7 | 학교와 학습 (School and study) | Describe study habits/methods; understand study problems | -기 쉽다/어렵다, -는 동안 | 3 sentences: an effective study method |
| 8 | 직장과 업무 (Work) | Understand work situations; understand schedule changes/requests | -기로 하다, -아/어야 되다 | A work schedule or to-do item |
| 9 | 교통과 도시생활 (Transit and city life) | Understand pros/cons of city life; describe traffic problems | -는데, -는 반면에 | 3-4 sentences: a city-life upside or inconvenience |
| 10 | 여행과 지역 (Travel and regions) | Describe a place's features; recommend a travel experience | -(으)면서, -기 좋다 | 3-4 sentences recommending a place |
| 11 | 문화와 생활 차이 (Cultural differences) | Understand cultural differences; compare two lifestyles | -에 비해서, -는 편이다 | A lifestyle difference one has experienced |
| 12 | 소비와 생활경제 (Spending) | Understand spending habits; explain price/choice reasons | -기보다는, -만큼 | 3-4 sentences: a way to save money |
| 13 | 미디어와 정보 (Media and information) | Understand information-seeking situations; state simple pros/cons | -다고 생각하다, -에 따르면 | A frequently used media/info source and why |
| 14 | 환경과 공공생활 (Environment, public life) | Understand environmental issues; understand public rules and why they exist | -아/어야 한다, -지 않으면 안 되다 | 3-4 sentences: something doable for the environment |
| 15 | 의견과 선택 (Opinions) | State one's own opinion with a simple reason; compare with another opinion | -다고 생각하다, -기 때문에, -지만 | 4-5 sentences of opinion on one topic — still not long-form essay writing |
| 16 | TOPIK 3 종합복습 (Review) | Comprehensive review + TOPIK 4 readiness check | Minimal new vocab/grammar | Short comprehensive writing, not new content |

STEP 16 mirrors TOPIK 1 STEP 12 / TOPIK 2 STEP 14's existing Review
STEP pattern: 0 (or near-0) new words, no new core grammar, heavier
Practice weighting, review across all of STEP 1-15's grammar/topics
(경험/계획/이유/비교/조건/문제-해결/의견/생활-사회 정보/Reading/
Listening/Writing), never a lock on TOPIK 4, no pass/certification
wording (same banned/allowed phrasing rules as the existing Review
STEPs).

## 4. Section sizing (general STEP; STEP 16 differs per §3)

- **Words**: ~18-22 new core words.
- **Sentences**: 6-8, a little longer than TOPIK 2's, mixing current +
  prior vocabulary/grammar, written so at least one or two are reusable
  as a Writing pattern.
- **Grammar**: 1-2 new points per STEP — TOPIK 3 leans on combining
  previously-learned grammar with the STEP's new point rather than
  teaching 3-4 new patterns at once.
- **Listening**: 3 questions. STEP 1-5: 3-4 sentence situations. STEP
  6-10: 4-5 sentences. STEP 11-15: 4-6 sentence dialogue/explanation.
  STEP 16: comprehensive. Questions should reach a little past literal
  facts into simple reason/intent, not just "what was said."
- **Reading**: 3 questions. STEP 1-5: 4-5 sentences. STEP 6-10: 5-7
  sentences. STEP 11-15: 6-8 sentences or a short info-style passage.
  STEP 16: comprehensive. A bit more inference than TOPIK 2, still far
  short of an argumentative passage.
- **Practice**: 10-12 questions, drawing from Vocabulary / Grammar /
  Sentence / Fill-blank / Situation / Listening-style / Reading /
  simple inference / a Writing-related multiple-choice item (sentence
  ordering, picking the right connector, etc. — Practice stays
  multiple-choice; it is not the Writing screen itself).

## 5. Writing sizing ramp

| STEPs | Length |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | 2-3 sentences |
| 5-8 | ~3 sentences |
| 9-12 | 3-4 sentences |
| 13-15 | 4-5 sentences |
| 16 | Short comprehensive writing, still not long-form |

## 6. Writing: structure direction (no implementation yet)

TOPIK 3 is where Writing starts, but it starts rule-based, not
AI-graded. No Writing screen, component, or WritingItem data file is
created this stage — this section only fixes the shape so later
stages build to a single agreed target instead of improvising one.

Recommended content forms (multiple-choice or short user-typed answer,
no free-form essay grading):

1. Sentence completion
2. Word-order arrangement
3. Write a sentence using given keyword(s)
4. Answer a question in 2-4 sentences
5. Show a short sample answer for self-check

Planned optional addition to `types/content.ts` (not made this stage):

```ts
export interface WritingItem {
  id: string;
  type: "sentence-completion" | "sentence-order" | "keyword-writing" | "short-response";
  prompt: string;
  keywords?: string[];
  sampleAnswer: string;
  explanation: Record<string, string>;
}
```

`StepContent.writing?: WritingItem[]` stays optional, exactly like the
placeholder already added in Stage 21 — TOPIK 1~2's 26 existing STEP
files are unaffected either way since none of them set `writing`.

**Explicitly not built now, and not planned for early TOPIK 3
either:** AI auto-grading, free-form essay scoring, TOPIK official
score prediction, AI grammar analysis. The only feedback loop for now
is: user writes → sees the sample answer → sees key points. AI Writing
is a later-stage decision, not part of this design.

## 7. TOPIK 2 → TOPIK 3 difficulty bridge

TOPIK 2 STEP 14 (review) must connect naturally into TOPIK 3 STEP 1 —
checked against TOPIK 2 STEP 14's actual content (Stage 20):

- STEP 14 already reviews -았/었어요, -(으)ㄹ 거예요, -고 있다, -(으)ㄹ
  수 있다/없다, 때문에, -아/어 보다, etc. TOPIK 3 STEP 1's -아/어 본
  적이 있다 extends -아/어 보다 (already reviewed) rather than
  introducing an unrelated pattern — a natural next step, not a jump.
- STEP 14's Reading/Listening already run 4-6 sentences (its own
  Review-STEP bump over the general TOPIK 2 STEP's 2-4). TOPIK 3 STEP
  1's target (4-5 sentence Listening, 4-5 sentence Reading) continues
  that length directly instead of jumping past it.
  TOPIK 3 STEP 1 must NOT open with long-form Reading, argumentative
  writing, advanced grammar, or abstract social vocabulary — confirmed
  by §1's out-of-scope list and STEP 1's actual topic (recent everyday
  experience, not an abstract one).
- Vocabulary: TOPIK 3 STEP 1 keeps reusing TOPIK 1~2 core words
  (사람/가족/친구/학교/시간/음식 etc., same words already reused
  across TOPIK 1→2 per the Stage 21 QA) rather than starting from an
  all-new list.

Future content-authoring stages for TOPIK 3 STEP 1 should verify this
bridge concretely (duplicate-check script + a fresh look at STEP 14's
actual sentences) before finalizing STEP 1's words/grammar, the same
way every past stage has cross-checked against the immediately
preceding STEP.

## 8. Cumulative vocabulary target

TOPIK 1~3 cumulative vocabulary DB target: **~3,000 words**. This is
not "16 STEPs × new-words-per-STEP" — it counts core learning
vocabulary + review words + the extra vocabulary that naturally
appears in Reading/Listening passages across all three levels, not
just each STEP's own new-word list.

## 9. Content-authoring rules fixed for future TOPIK 3 stages

Carried over unchanged from `docs/content-rules.md`, plus what's new
here:

- Never re-register an existing TOPIK 1/2 "new" word as "new" again in
  TOPIK 3 — reuse it with `status: "review"`, per the existing rule.
  Run the same duplicate-check script (pull every "new" Korean word
  across all existing STEP files) before designing each new STEP's
  word list, now against TOPIK 1+2+3-so-far combined.
- Grammar: before assigning a STEP's "new" grammar pattern, check it
  hasn't already been introduced as new anywhere in TOPIK 1~2 or an
  earlier TOPIK 3 STEP (this is exactly the class of bug Stage 21's QA
  found and fixed) — reuse as "(복습)" instead.
  Keep 1-2 new grammar points per STEP; do not stack 3-4.
- `relatedWords` must only reference vocabulary actually taught
  somewhere (any STEP, any level) — never a bare function word that
  was never introduced as a Word entry.
- `correctAnswer` stays 0-based project-wide, and answer positions
  should be checked for skew and rebalanced by reordering options
  (never by rewriting the question) before a stage is considered done.
- STEP 16 follows the same Review-STEP rules already established for
  TOPIK 1 STEP 12 and TOPIK 2 STEP 14: minimal/zero new words, no new
  core grammar, heavier Practice weighting, no lock on the next Level,
  no pass/fail/certification wording (banned: "Passed/Failed/
  Certified/Official TOPIK 3", "You are TOPIK Level 3"; allowed:
  "Review Result", "Learning Readiness", "Suggested Next Step",
  "Ready to Try TOPIK 4", "More Review Recommended").
- Writing content, once actually authored, follows §4-§6's sizing ramp
  and stays rule-based (sample answer + key points) — no AI grading is
  introduced without a separate, explicit decision.
